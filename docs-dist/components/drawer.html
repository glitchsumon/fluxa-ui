Drawer

Slide-in panels for filters, detail views and secondary nav. Because they share the overlay engine with modals, drawers stack, trap focus and lock the page scroll.

Demo

<button data-fx-drawer="filters">Open drawer</button>

Edges

fx-drawer-left and fx-drawer-right — full-height rails.
fx-drawer-top / fx-drawer-bottom — horizontal rails.
Override panel width on .fx-drawer-panel inline or via your theme.
